Our story.

Creative IQ brings hands-on STEM education to children through robotics, engineering, coding and community partnerships.

Our values

Six values guide what we teach and how we work.

Why we exist

Creative IQ began with the belief that postcode, background and starting point should never decide who gets to try STEM. We bring practical projects and supportive mentors closer to families through school sessions, charity partnerships and local workshops.

Our mission

We help children build, create and lead. Through our learning centres and partnerships, children practise solving real problems and build the confidence to shape the world around them.

Sustainability and ethics

We reuse materials, reduce waste and choose kits designed for repeated use. Our commitment also shapes how we hire and how we create safe, respectful places to learn.

Equality and inclusivity

Children of every background, ability and identity deserve to feel welcome. We make room for individual needs and help each child grow in confidence.

What we believe

  • Inclusivity — welcome children from every background.
  • Equality — give every child access to opportunities to learn.
  • Community — build relationships that continue beyond lessons.
  • Sustainability — make learning and materials go further.
  • Curiosity — make room for questions and experiments.
  • Excellence — keep improving the experience we offer.
