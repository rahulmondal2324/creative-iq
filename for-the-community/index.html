For the Community.

STEM starts close to home.

Creative IQ works with local schools and community organisations to give more children time, space and support to explore STEM.

Charity partnerships

We work with local charities to make STEM opportunities available to children who have had less access to them.

Free STEM workshops

Free workshops and trial classes give children an opportunity to build, experiment and get to know STEM.

Local school engagement

We work with nearby schools to bring practical STEM projects into lessons and support teachers with real-world activities.
