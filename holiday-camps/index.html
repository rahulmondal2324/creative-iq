Holiday Camps.

Build something this school break.

Try robotics, coding and team challenges in mentor-led holiday sessions with separate junior and senior groups.

What does a day look like?

Children build and test robots, solve design challenges and work in teams. First-timers are welcome, and returning campers can try different projects.

Parent & Instructor FAQs.

Booking & Enrolment

How do I book a STEM robotics or coding course for my child?

Choose the age group, course and venue on the Creative IQ booking page, then follow the checkout steps.

Can I book more than one child at the same time?

Yes. Add each child’s details at checkout to book them together.

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Use SIBLING10 at checkout for 10% off a sibling’s course booking.

Can my child join mid-term?

Yes, when places are available. The team checks your child’s skills to help them start at a suitable level.

How much do courses cost?

Fees depend on the venue, programme and duration. See the listed price on the Creative IQ booking page.

Trial Sessions

Do you offer trial sessions?

The Creative IQ booking page shows which venues offer free or paid trials.

What happens during a trial?

An instructor introduces the kit, helps your child build a robot and gives them time to test it.

Will I get feedback after a trial?

Instructors give verbal and written feedback on the build and how your child approached it.

Courses vs. Clubs

How are the courses different from school clubs?

The courses follow RoboThink’s eight-level curriculum. Projects build on earlier lessons as children develop their skills.

What skills can my child learn?

Depending on the course, children can practise robotics, programming, engineering design, game creation and teamwork.

Do you offer AI lessons?

Age-appropriate AI activities are included in advanced engineering and coding tracks.

At what point can children start learning AI?

Creative IQ introduces AI after students complete advanced engineering levels or Coding Lab courses.

Student Progress

How will I know how my child is doing?

Children receive written feedback after each lesson. Creative IQ also shares photos and videos privately with parents.

How often are students assessed?

Every sixth lesson reviews the skills covered in the previous five.

How can we use the written feedback?

Keep the feedback sheets together to build a record of your child’s STEM projects and progress.

Do children earn certificates?

Yes. Creative IQ awards a certificate for each level completed.

How long does the engineering pathway take?

The eight levels typically take over three years. Students can progress at their own pace.

STEM Competitions

When can students qualify for competitions?

Students can enter the qualification stage after completing at least two terms.

Where are competitions held?

Events may take place at RoboThink Discovery Centres, schools or universities. Families receive venue details in advance.

Workshops

Who can take part in STEM workshops?

Workshops welcome new and returning students. See the community page for school and community partnerships.

Should my child bring lunch?

For the workshops described on the homepage, children only need a nut-free snack and a drink.

Robotics

Does my child need to bring equipment?

Creative IQ supplies robotics kits, tools and materials. Laptops are provided for Coding Lab sessions.

What if my child already has robotics experience?

Instructors assess new students and adapt projects to keep them appropriately challenged.

Safety

Are instructors trained?

Creative IQ says instructors have enhanced DBS checks and safeguarding training. See its safeguarding policy.

What is the instructor-to-student ratio?

Creative IQ lists one instructor for every five students.

Cancellations

What happens if I need to cancel or change a booking?

See Creative IQ’s refund and cancellation policy and contact the team about your booking.

What if Creative IQ cancels a session?

Creative IQ says it will arrange a replacement session or offer a credit. See the cancellation policy for details.

Student Engagement

What if my child isn’t enjoying the lessons?

Speak with the team. They can discuss what your child enjoys and adapt the course where possible.

Can children get individual guidance?

Instructors use small classes to adapt projects to each child’s experience and interests.

Why does motivation matter in STEM?

Each level adds harder challenges. Staying engaged helps children practise problem-solving and grow in confidence.
