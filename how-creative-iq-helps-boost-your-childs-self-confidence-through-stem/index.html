Building confidence through STEM.

How completing a project, trying again and sharing what worked can help children build self-belief.

Read the full article ↗

Show children what they can do

Building, coding and troubleshooting a robot lets a child see the result of their own decisions. A mistake becomes another chance to test an idea.

Adapt the challenge

Creative IQ describes small groups and personalised guidance so children can work on projects that suit their experience and pace.

Make achievements visible

Written feedback and certificates give children a record of the skills they’ve gained. Sharing a project with family gives them a chance to explain how it works.
