Our Courses.

From first builds to big ideas.

Find the right starting point in robotics, engineering, game design and coding.

Junior Engineer ↓Engineer Level ↓Advanced Engineer ↓Coding & AI ↓

Ages 4–7 · 90 minutes · 6 terms

Junior Engineer

Children build moving, responsive robots and get started with visual coding through hands-on, screen-free challenges.

  • Build robots that move, respond and light up
  • Try age-appropriate block coding
  • Practise fine motor skills, teamwork and persistence
Enrol in Junior Engineer ↗

Ages 8–14 · 90 minutes

Engineer Level

Students put robotics, coding and game design together while building the skills they need for the Advanced Engineer track.

  • Build with motors, gears and sensors
  • Design games and animations
  • Practise working independently and in teams
Enrol in Engineer Level ↗

Advanced Engineer

After completing Engineer Level, students move into electronics, hardware integration and independent builds.

  • Explore circuits, inputs, outputs and real-time response
  • Use RoComi to control servo motors and automate projects
  • Use RoCode to link block-based code to hardware and control games

Ages 8–14 · 90 minutes · 9 terms

Coding & AI

A screen-based pathway for children who want to design games, learn Python and explore AI through practical projects.

  • Video game design and interactive storytelling
  • Programming fundamentals in Python
  • AI activities and algorithmic problem-solving
